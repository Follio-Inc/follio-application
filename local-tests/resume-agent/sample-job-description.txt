Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform
Northwind Robotics (Remote, US)

About the team
We keep money moving for a few thousand merchants. The payments platform team
owns settlement, refunds and the ledger that everything reconciles against.

What you'll do
Design and run backend services that handle settlement and refunds at volume.
Own a service end to end: schema, rollout, on-call, and the postmortem when it
goes wrong. Work with the data team on reporting pipelines.

Requirements
- Strong Python. Most of the platform is Python and we expect you to be fluent.
- Production PostgreSQL experience: query tuning, indexing, migrations.
- Experience with Kubernetes. Our services run on EKS and you will be expected
  to debug your own deployments.
- Familiarity with Terraform for infrastructure changes.
- Comfortable carrying a pager for services you own.

Nice to have
- Experience with Kafka or another event streaming platform.
- Exposure to GraphQL — we are migrating parts of the public API.
- Prior work in payments, billing or another regulated money domain.

Benefits
Medical, dental and vision from day one. Flexible PTO, a home office stipend,
and an annual learning budget. We are an equal opportunity employer and
consider all qualified applicants without regard to protected status.
